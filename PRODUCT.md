# Product

## Register

product

## Users

FlexMed has two primary user groups: customers browsing the research catalog and founder/admin users managing orders, inventory, supply purchases, fulfillment, client history, affiliates, vial cases, product assets, and COAs. Customers need a fast, clear shopping workflow with accurate availability and documentation. Founder/admin users need dense, dependable tools that keep shared Supabase data aligned across devices.

## Product Purpose

FlexMed is a research-catalog storefront and operating dashboard. Success means customers can browse listings, create accounts, place orders, and receive fulfillment updates without confusion, while the founder can maintain inventory, products, strengths, supply orders, COAs, shipment details, and customer records from one shared source of truth.

## Brand Personality

Clinical, organized, and founder-operated. The interface should feel trustworthy and efficient without becoming cold or overcomplicated.

## Anti-references

Avoid decorative marketplace clutter, slow all-at-once product rendering, unclear stock labels, duplicated product listings for strength variants, overdesigned admin cards, and workflows that hide whether data saved locally or synced to Supabase.

## Design Principles

- Make the source of truth visible: shared inventory, order, COA, and customer data should clearly show what is live.
- Keep founder workflows fast: repeated tasks like inventory editing, fulfillment, and supply tracking should be compact and low-friction.
- Customers should never have to decode the catalog: product families, strengths, stock state, COA availability, and checkout steps need to be obvious.
- Prefer reliable manual workflows first, then automate: OCR and other optimizations should assist without blocking orders.
- Performance is part of trust: client pages should feel responsive even with a large catalog and uploaded assets.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Aim for WCAG AA contrast, keyboard-accessible controls, readable dense admin layouts, visible focus states, and reduced-motion-friendly interactions.
